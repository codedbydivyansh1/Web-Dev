About the Instructors

Rohit Negi

Rohit Negi is a passionate software engineer, educator, and founder of Coder Army. An IIT Guwahati graduate and GATE AIR 202 ranker, he has cracked top tech offers including Uber. Coming from a humble farming background, Rohit now dedicates himself to empowering students through high-quality, free coding content. His teaching covers Data Structures & Algorithms, System Design, Java, Spring Boot, and full-stack web development. Currently, he is running a live MERN Stack + Java + Spring Boot course on YouTube, designed to help students build real-world projects and become job-ready. His mission is simple: make quality tech education accessible to all.

Aditya Tandon

Aditya Tandon is a skilled software engineer, educator, and Co-Founder of Coder Army. He holds an M.Tech in Computer Science from IIT Guwahati and has worked as a Senior Software Engineer at Ola. With deep expertise in backend development, Java, Spring Boot, and scalable system design, Aditya brings real-world experience into the classroom. He actively mentors students through free, high-quality content on YouTube, focusing on full-stack development and system design. Known for his clear, structured teaching style, Aditya helps learners build strong technical foundations and industry-ready skills. His mission is to bridge the gap between education and employability.
